Notes

Threshold-day counts are scaled up for missing days; years with under ~77% of days observed are excluded. Rain = average of the nearest FCDMC gauges (Chandler has 2, Gilbert 4, most others 4-6); calendar years. The volunteer (CoCoRaHS/co-op) gauges read 6-30% wetter than the county gauges in most areas, so treat rain as a range. Elevation matters as much as latitude here: higher areas (North Scottsdale, Carefree, Fountain Hills) run several degrees cooler.
